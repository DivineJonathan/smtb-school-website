Our Programmes

Four Stages, One Vision

Each level is designed to meet the developmental needs of its age group while building toward a unified vision of the educated Christian young person.

Ages 3–5

Nursery

Play-based learning, phonics, number sense, and social-emotional growth in a joyful, nurturing environment.
Ages 6–11

Primary

Strong literacy and numeracy foundations, science, humanities, the arts, and daily Christian formation.
Ages 12–14

Junior Secondary

Deeper subject specialisation, critical thinking, independent research, and the house system for character.
Ages 15–17

Senior Secondary

WAEC and IGCSE pathways, university guidance, leadership programme, and the senior thesis project.
Curriculum

What We Teach

Explore the subjects and disciplines that shape our students' minds and hearts.

Chemistry

From atomic theory to organic synthesis — practical lab work underpins every concept.

Physics

Mechanics, electricity, waves, and modern physics — taught with rigour and real-world application.

Biology

From cellular biology to ecology — understanding life as God's handiwork, with wonder intact.

Mathematics

From arithmetic to calculus — building logical reasoning and quantitative fluency at every level.

Computer Science

Coding, robotics, algorithms, and computational thinking — preparing students for a digital world.

Further Maths

An optional pathway for our most able mathematicians, preparing for STEM degrees at top universities.

English Literature

From Shakespeare to African novelists — reading with understanding, writing with clarity and power.

History

African, European, and world history — understanding the past to navigate the present with wisdom.

Geography

Physical and human geography — exploring God's world, its systems, and our responsibility to it.

Government

Political systems, civic responsibility, and the Christian's role in public life and citizenship.

Economics

Microeconomics, macroeconomics, and development — understanding stewardship of resources.

French

Language proficiency and cultural literacy — opening doors across francophone Africa and beyond.

Visual Arts

Drawing, painting, sculpture, and digital art — cultivating creativity and aesthetic discernment.

Music

Choir, orchestra, music theory, and individual instrumental tuition from specialist teachers.

Drama

Performance, improvisation, and technical theatre — building confidence, voice, and presence.

Design & Technology

Product design, engineering graphics, and hands-on making in our dedicated DT workshop.

Biblical Studies

Old and New Testament survey, biblical theology, and apologetics — grounding faith in Scripture.

Daily Worship

Morning chapel, hymn singing, prayer, and Scripture reading — the heartbeat of our community.

Service Learning

Community outreach, missions, and social responsibility — putting faith into action locally and beyond.

Christian Ethics

Moral reasoning from a Christian worldview — engaging contemporary issues with wisdom and grace.

Lower School

Foundations for a Lifetime

In the early years, we build the habits of mind and heart that everything else depends upon — curiosity, discipline, kindness, and a love of learning that does not fade.

  • Phonics, numeracy, and inquiry-based discovery
  • Small class sizes with dedicated specialist teachers
  • Daily worship and Christian formation
Upper School

Preparation for the World's Best

Our senior students pursue a rigorous programme of sciences, humanities, and the arts — graduating as confident, articulate young adults ready to lead and serve.

  • WAEC, IGCSE, and university-track pathways
  • Advanced laboratories, studios, and debate societies
  • Dedicated university and careers guidance
