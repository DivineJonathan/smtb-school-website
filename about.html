Our Mission

To Educate, Equip, and Send Forth

SMTB School exists to provide a Christ-centred education that cultivates intellectual excellence, godly character, and a lifelong love of learning — preparing young people to lead and serve in their generation.

We are committed to nurturing the whole child: mind, body, and spirit. Every lesson, every activity, and every relationship is an opportunity to shape a life of purpose.

Our Vision

A Generation of Faithful Leaders

We envision graduates who are intellectually robust, morally anchored, and spiritually alive — young men and women who carry the light of Christ into every sphere of influence they enter.

Our vision is not merely to produce excellent students, but to form excellent human beings — people of wisdom, courage, and compassion.

Our Journey

A History of Faithful Stewardship

From a small classroom of twelve to a thriving community of over 1,200 students — this is the story God has written through SMTB.

1998

The Beginning

SMTB School opens its doors with twelve students and two teachers in a converted parish hall, founded on a vision for Christ-centred education.

2005

Campus Expansion

Having outgrown its original home, the school moves to its current 15-acre campus — purpose-built for discovery, worship, and learning.

2012

Senior Secondary Launch

Our first senior secondary class begins, extending the SMTB journey through to university preparation and opening doors to the world's best institutions.

2018

20th Anniversary

Over 500 graduates have now passed through our gates, studying at universities across Nigeria, the UK, the US, and Canada. A milestone of God's faithfulness.

2024

A New Chapter

With record exam results, a growing faculty, and expanded facilities, SMTB stands ready for its next quarter century of shaping faithful, excellent young people.

What We Believe

Our Core Values

Four principles that are not slogans on a wall but lived realities in our daily life together.

Faith

Christ at the centre of all things — the source and summit of our life together.

Integrity

Truthfulness in speech, honour in conduct, faithfulness in small and great things.

Excellence

Doing all things well — as unto the Lord — because sloth dishonours the Giver of gifts.

Service

Leadership as servanthood — using one's gifts for the good of others and the glory of God.
