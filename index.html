A Word of Welcome

Where Every Child Is Known, Loved, and Challenged

At SMTB School, we believe education is far more than the transmission of knowledge. It is the cultivation of soul, mind, and character — a sacred trust we hold with every family that walks through our gates.

"We do not merely prepare students for examinations; we prepare them for life — to think clearly, act honourably, and live faithfully."

For over two decades, our community has been shaped by a simple conviction: that young people flourish when they are known deeply, challenged appropriately, and surrounded by adults who love them. That is the SMTB difference.

Dr. Samuel Adeyemi
Principal, SMTB School

Our Foundation

Core Values That Shape Everything

Four enduring principles guide our community — woven through every lesson, every relationship, and every decision.

Faith

A Christ-centred foundation where students discover purpose, hope, and a lifelong relationship with God.

Integrity

We cultivate honesty, courage, and moral clarity — the backbone of character that endures beyond the classroom.

Excellence

We pursue the highest standard in all things — not for praise, but because doing things well honours God and serves others.

Service

Leadership begins with serving others. Our students learn that their gifts are entrusted to them for the good of their community.

The SMTB Difference

Why Families Choose SMTB

Six reasons discerning families entrust their children to our care — year after year, generation after generation.

Christian Worldview

Every subject is taught through the lens of a Christian understanding of truth, beauty, and purpose.

Academic Rigour

A demanding curriculum that produces consistently outstanding examination results and university placements.

Small Classes

With a maximum of 18 students per class, every child is truly known, supported, and stretched.

World-Class Facilities

Science laboratories, art studios, libraries, sports complex, and technology-rich classrooms.

Character Formation

House system, leadership programme, and service initiatives that forge resilient, honourable young people.

Caring Community

A family atmosphere where teachers, parents, and students walk together through every season of growth.

Our Schools

A Journey from First Steps to Final Exams

Four distinct stages, one continuous vision — each building on the last, each with its own character and care.

Ages 3–5

Nursery

A joyful, play-based beginning where curiosity, language, and friendship take root.
Ages 6–11

Primary

Strong foundations in literacy, numeracy, and character — with wonder intact.
Ages 12–14

Junior Secondary

A bridge to deeper study — where independence, argument, and craft begin to grow.
Ages 15–17

Senior Secondary

University-track rigour, leadership, and the final shaping of a life of purpose.
